EASIEST WAY TO LAUNCH (ONE CLICK):
====================================================================
Double-click "START_PORTFOLIO.bat" in d:\portfolio
This starts both backend and frontend servers AND automatically opens
http://localhost:3000/ in your browser.

OR Launch Manually:
Step 1: Double-click "run_backend.bat"  (Starts Django on port 8000)
Step 2: Double-click "run_frontend.bat" (Starts React on port 3000)
Step 3: Open http://localhost:3000/ in your browser!

Note: In VS Code / Antigravity editor, hold 'Ctrl' while clicking
links (Ctrl + Click) to open them in your browser.
